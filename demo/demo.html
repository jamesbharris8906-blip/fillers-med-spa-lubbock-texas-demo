Botox

Botox relaxes the small muscles that crease the skin when you frown, smile, or raise your brows. It is a quick visit, and the change builds over a few days. Many people pair it with fillers, because Botox smooths lines that move, while fillers add volume where it has faded. Together, they can give a soft, balanced look.

Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers add volume to cheeks, lips, jawlines, and smile lines, and they can soften hollows under the eyes. The team places each small amount with care, checks your face while you move, and aims for a natural result. Most people see a change right away, and the look softens over the next few days.

XERF

XERF uses radiofrequency energy to warm the deeper layers of skin and help collagen grow. It can help skin feel firmer over time, without surgery or a long recovery. The treatment feels warm and easy, and it pairs well with fillers for people who want both volume and firmer skin.

LaseMD Ultra

LaseMD Ultra is a gentle laser that helps with dark spots, rough texture, and dull tone. It can brighten the skin and help serums sink in better after the treatment. Most people have some redness for a day, then the skin starts to look fresh and clear.

Potenza RF Microneedling

Potenza RF Microneedling pairs tiny needles with radiofrequency energy to help skin rebuild collagen. It can help with fine lines, acne scars, large pores, and skin that feels loose. A series of visits often gives the best result, and the change builds over weeks.

PicoSure Pro

PicoSure Pro uses very fast pulses of light to fade dark spots and even out skin tone. It can also help with fine lines and texture. The visit is quick, downtime is short, and skin can look clearer over a few sessions. It is a good match for skin that has seen a lot of Lubbock sun.

Keralase

Keralase is a treatment for hair and scalp health. A laser helps the scalp take in a serum that supports thicker, fuller-looking hair. The visit is calm, with no cuts and no long recovery. People who notice thinning hair often ask about it during a filler visit.

Titanium Laser Hair Removal

Titanium Laser Hair Removal helps cut down on unwanted hair with a fast, modern laser. It can treat larger areas in less time, and a cooling tip keeps the skin calm. A series of sessions is needed, because hair grows in cycles, and many people choose it to save time on shaving.

Skin Boosters

Skin Boosters are small injections that add deep hydration across a wide area of skin. They help skin look plump, bright, and soft, and they work well with fillers. While fillers add volume in one spot, skin boosters improve moisture and glow all over, and the result can last for months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a best age to start fillers?

There is no one best age. Some people start in their thirties, when they first notice volume loss. Others start later, when lines and hollows are more clear. The team looks at your skin, your goals, and your health, not just your age. A good plan is one that fits you, whenever you start.

Will fillers affect my smile or my face when I talk?

A good filler should not change how you smile or talk. The team checks your face while you move, and they place the gel so it moves with you. If a spot is treated too much, a smile can look stiff, which is why a light hand matters. Tell the team if your smile is a worry.

How can I tell if my filler has settled well?

Give it a week. Swelling fades, the gel softens, and the look becomes more natural. Take a photo before your visit and one a week after, so you can see the change. If a spot looks uneven after a week, call the team. They can see you for a follow-up and talk through a small touch-up.

Do I need to stop any medicines before fillers?

Some medicines and supplements can add bruising, so tell the team everything you take. They will talk through what to pause, if anything, and what is safe to keep taking. Do not stop a medicine your doctor gave you without asking that doctor first. Bring a list with you to your visit.

Can fillers be used on my hands?

Yes, fillers can add volume to the backs of hands, where veins and tendons can show more with age. The team will talk with you about whether this spot fits your goals, and what to expect. Hands often need a different plan than the face, so a talk first is a good idea.

What should I eat or drink after my visit?

Eat a normal meal, drink water, and avoid alcohol for a day. There is no special diet after fillers. The main steps are simple: ice if you have swelling, be gentle with the treated spots, and skip hard workouts for a day. The team will give you a short list to take home.
